# Turkish & Central Anatolian Kurdish | AI Language Evaluation

## Project Overview
This independent AI language evaluation project focuses on Turkish–Kurdish code-switching, with particular attention to Kurdish varieties spoken in Central Anatolia, Türkiye.

The project explores how AI models interpret regional vocabulary, everyday expressions, mixed-language conversations, and context-dependent meanings.

Its purpose is to identify potential misunderstandings and document model responses through exploratory human evaluation. Central Anatolian Kurdish is treated as a regional linguistic context rather than a single standardized dialect.

## Objectives
- Identify misunderstandings in mixed-language conversations.
- Evaluate whether AI responses preserve the intended meaning.
- Examine regional vocabulary and contextual ambiguity.
- Document errors and suggest more accurate interpretations.

## Evaluation Method
The planned evaluation process includes collecting original or permission-cleared examples, testing AI responses, comparing interpretations with verified meanings, and documenting error categories.

## Current Status
Two initial exploratory AI language evaluation tests have been published (TEST-001 and TEST-002). These preliminary results document observed model responses and evaluator judgments. They do not establish general model performance or comprehensive coverage of Central Anatolian Kurdish varieties.
